(i) Agriculture:
The practice and science of cultivating the soil, growing crops, and raising livestock.
(ii) Soil:
The topmost loose layer of the earth's crust that supports plant growth and provides anchorage and
nutrients.
(iii) Soil Horizon:
A distinct parallel layer in the soil pro?le possessing physical and chemical characteristics di?erent from
adjacent layers.
(iv) Illuviation:
The accumulation or deposition of leached mineral particles and organic matter in lower soil horizons
(e.g., B-horizon).
(v) Eluviation:
The washing out or leaching of mineral nutrients, clay, and organic matter from upper soil horizons (e.g.,
A-horizon) by percolating water.
